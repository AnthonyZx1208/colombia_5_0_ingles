Purpose

A project for organizing technical ideas with academic criteria

The objective of this website is to explain, in a clear way, how the topics discussed at Colombia 5.0 relate to the education of a systems engineering student. The page does not only present information; it also organizes concepts, compares ideas, and proposes a reflection on the impact of technology on companies and society.

The main focus is video game UI design with Figma. From that topic, the site analyzes reusable components, variants, Auto Layout, design systems, and the preparation of interfaces for future tools based on artificial intelligence.

Conferences

Two perspectives for understanding current technology

Talk 01

Video game UI with Figma

Speaker: Presenter from the Colombia 5.0 event.

Topic: Building reusable, scalable, and easy-to-edit interfaces.

The talk shows that a video game interface should not be improvised screen by screen. When a project uses components, frames, variants, and Auto Layout, the design can grow without losing consistency. This matters because video games usually need menus, buttons, indicators, health bars, and panels that change according to the user's context.

The 9-slice technique is also valuable because it makes it possible to resize containers without deforming their corners. Together, these practices help teams work faster and with fewer visual errors.

Key idea: a good design system prepares a project to scale and to integrate better with AI tools.

Talk 02

Ethics, AI, and digital companies

Speaker: Complementary academic analysis.

Topic: Responsibility in the use of data, automation, and artificial intelligence.

Business technology can improve processes, reduce time, and support decisions, but it can also create risks when used without control. For that reason, artificial intelligence should be applied with transparency, human supervision, and respect for data privacy.

For a future systems engineer, programming is not only writing code. It also means thinking about security, accessibility, fairness, social impact, and the consequences for the people who will use the system.

Key idea: a technological solution should be useful, but also responsible, secure, and understandable.

Ethical Conclusion

Business technology needs human judgment

Ethics is implemented when companies design technology while considering the consequences of their decisions. In projects with artificial intelligence, this means reviewing the data used, explaining results, avoiding discrimination, and maintaining human supervision.

Automation should not be seen as a way to remove responsibility. Even when a system performs tasks automatically, developers and organizations remain responsible for its behavior, security, and social impact.

As a second-semester student, I believe that learning to program also requires learning to make responsible decisions. Quality software should work well, but it should also protect privacy, accessibility, and user trust.
